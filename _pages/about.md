## About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He. I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2024–Present). I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. (2020–2024). Professor Junxian He previously advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
